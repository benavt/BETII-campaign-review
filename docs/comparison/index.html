Scope and metric definitions

This page retains the original ten-design panel (nine candidates plus AP2-9) and exactly the ten records from the supplied modified Adaptyv FASTA. The original panel includes one collaborator-origin candidate; grouping here follows the requested panels, not design origin. AP2-9 remains a requested reference; its experimental positive-control status has not been verified.

Native confidence metrics belong to the selected model, method and isoform. Changing the folding method never carries over Kds from another structure. Minimum selectivity always compares BRD2, BRD4 and BRDT to BRD3 on Boltz2 Exact coordinates, regardless of the selected display isoform. ipSAE core and full-chain values remain separate. Native confidence score definitions can differ by method.

NetSolP-D solubility and purification usability are sequence-level scores from 0 to 1, independent of the selected complex. Working pocket distance is available only where a source-bound assessment exists. Homodimer ranks are native ranks within each condition, not matched conformations between sequences.

DeltaForge has 19 complete panels in this selection. miniprotein_879 is pending BRD2, BRD4 and BRDT Kds. PCANN has ten complete Jokent panels; the original panel has no saved PCANN predictions. Unavailable values are never replaced with another scorer. Group medians describe available rows only and do not establish experimental performance.

This page reads published saved evidence; it launches no folding, predictions or paid requests.
